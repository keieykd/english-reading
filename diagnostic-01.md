## Passage A — 新聞

Starting next month, the city of Lindfield will make all municipal buses free to ride. The three-year trial, approved by the city council in a narrow 6-5 vote, is intended to reduce traffic in the historic centre, where car journeys have risen by nearly a fifth since 2019.

Supporters argue that the scheme will pay for itself. Fares currently cover only 14 per cent of the network's operating costs, and the council expects savings from removing ticket machines and fare inspectors to offset much of the lost revenue. Critics counter that the money would be better spent on the routes themselves, several of which run only once an hour after seven in the evening.

Similar experiments elsewhere have produced mixed results. Ridership almost always increases, but studies suggest that most new passengers are people who would otherwise have walked or cycled, rather than drivers persuaded to leave their cars at home.

(a) Why buses in Lindfield are unreliable in the evening
(b) A city's plan to abolish bus fares, and the arguments on both sides
(c) How removing ticket machines reduces a city's operating costs
(d) A study comparing walking, cycling and driving

**A2.** According to the passage, ticket fares currently cover what share of the network's operating costs?
(a) 6 per cent　(b) 14 per cent　(c) 19 per cent　(d) 20 per cent

**A3.** What does the final paragraph imply about schemes of this kind?
(a) They rarely attract more passengers
(b) They may fail to achieve their main purpose
(c) They are usually cheaper to run than expected
(d) They are opposed by most transport researchers

## Passage B — 職場通知（多益 Part 7 型）

MEMORANDUM
To: All Wilmot Tower staff
From: Facilities Management
Re: Parking arrangements during resurfacing work

Resurfacing of the underground car park will begin on Monday, 14 October, and is expected to take four weeks. During this period Levels B2 and B3 will be closed. Level B1 will remain open but is reserved for visitors and for staff holding accessible-parking permits.

All other permit holders should use the Eastgate public car park on Renner Street. The company has arranged a discounted rate of $4 per day, which is applied automatically when you scan your staff badge at the exit barrier. Please note that the discount cannot be claimed retroactively, so badges must be scanned at the time of departure.

Staff who normally arrive before 7:30 a.m. may find the Renner Street entrance still closed; in that case, please use the Halliday Avenue entrance, which operates twenty-four hours.

(d) The fee is automatically deducted from their salary

Fare Conditions - "Saver Flex"

Tickets purchased under the Saver Flex fare may be changed once without charge, provided the change is requested no later than 24 hours before the scheduled departure of the first flight in the itinerary, and the new travel date falls within twelve months of the original date of issue. Where the new booking is in a higher fare class, the difference must be paid at the time of the change; no refund is given where the new booking is in a lower class.

Cancellations made more than seven days before departure are refunded in full, less an administrative fee of EUR 45. Thereafter, only government taxes and airport charges are refundable. Passengers who fail to present themselves at the gate by the stated boarding time are treated as no-shows, and all remaining sectors of the itinerary are cancelled automatically, unless the airline is notified of the intended interruption at least two hours prior to the missed departure.
