English Reading — 泰國實戰包

2026/09/10 – 09/11 · 離線可讀 · 站著也能看

用法：在泰國現場直接翻這頁。句子短、加粗的直接唸。 不確定怎麼說的時候，一律用 Could I …? 或 Could you …?

① 入境

Purpose of visit?

Tourism.

How long will you be staying?

Four nights.

Where are you staying?

At the Ibis Bangkok Riverside. I have the booking here.

Do you have a return ticket?

Yes, I fly back on Tuesday.

指標：Passport Control／Immigration（查驗）· Baggage Claim（領行李）· Nothing to Declare 綠線／Goods to Declare 紅線（海關）

② 計程車 / Grab

Could you use the meter, please?

How much to Sukhumvit, roughly?

Could you drop me off at the corner?

Sorry, I think we’ve missed the turning.

Grab 事先顯示車資，直接省掉整場議價。

③ 飯店

I have a reservation under Chen.

Here you go. （遞護照）

Is breakfast included?

Could I have a late check-out?

Could I leave my bags here until five?

④ 餐廳

Table for two, please.

Could we have a menu in English, please?

What would you recommend?

Is this spicy?

No chilli at all, please.

Does this contain peanuts?

Could we get the bill, please?

Can I pay by card?

陷阱：泰國餐廳的 mild 跟你的 mild 不是同一件事。 真的不吃辣就說 No chilli at all, please.

⑤ 夜市殺價

How much is this?

That’s a bit more than I wanted to spend.

Would you take three hundred?

What’s your best price?

I’ll take two. Any discount?

Could you throw in one of those?

I’ll think about it, thanks. （走人，常會被叫回來）

市集可以殺，百貨與便利商店不行。語氣比用字重要：笑著說，願意走。

⑥ 商店 / 藥局

Do you have a plug adapter?

Could I get a bag, please? （泰國塑膠袋要另外要）

Do you take card?

I’ve got an upset stomach. （腸胃不適）

Do you have anything for motion sickness?

Do you have something for the itching?

Is this drowsy or non-drowsy?

How many times a day should I take it?

標示意思
twice daily一天兩次
after meals飯後
do not exceed the stated dose不可超過建議劑量
may cause drowsiness可能想睡
藥師看寫的比聽的準。打在手機上給他看最快。

⑦ 出狀況

Sorry, I think there’s a mistake on the bill.

I was charged twice for this.

We didn’t order this.

I think I left my bag in the taxi.

I’ve lost my phone. Is there a lost and found here?

Sorry, I don’t understand. Could you say that again, more slowly?

Could you write it down?

Could you help me, please?

I need to get to a hospital.

Does anyone here speak English?

原則：出狀況時句子要短。慌的時候長句一定垮，短句才說得出口。

🎒 蒐集任務

拍下 3–5 個看不懂或要想一下的英文：告示、菜單、機場指示、飯店規則、藥品說明。

不計出席、不計缺席。回來一起拆，直接變成第 2 週教材。

Day 1 — 9/10（四）抵達與移動

通勤泛讀規則：不查字典、不求全懂。看不懂就跳過，把整塊讀完。
CHUNK 1 · 約 4 分鐘

入境卡與海關

Thailand no longer requires most visitors to complete a paper arrival card, but you should still be ready to answer the same questions at the counter. Officers ask them quickly and they rarely repeat themselves.

Purpose of visit?

Tourism.

How long will you be staying?

Four nights.

Where are you staying?

At the Ibis Bangkok Riverside. I have the booking here.

  • Passport Control / Immigration — 查驗護照
  • Thai Nationals vs Foreign Passports — 站錯排會被請回去
  • Baggage Claim — 領行李
  • Nothing to Declare（綠）vs Goods to Declare（紅）
“Please have your passport and boarding pass ready.”
have something ready = 先拿在手上，不是「擁有」。
CHUNK 2 · 約 5 分鐘

訂房確認信 （細節都藏在中段）

Dear Mr Chen,

Thank you for choosing Ibis Bangkok Riverside. Your reservation is confirmed.

Check-in: from 14:00 on Saturday 12 September
Check-out: by 12:00 on Tuesday 15 September
Room: 1 Standard Twin, non-smoking, breakfast included

Early check-in is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed. If you expect to arrive after 18:00, please inform us in advance; otherwise the room may be released. A refundable deposit of THB 1,000 will be taken at check-in and returned on departure, provided the room is left in good condition.

Free cancellation until 23:59 on 10 September. After that, the first night will be charged.
  • subject to availability — 看有沒有空房 ＝ 不保證
  • released — 房間被釋出給別人，你的訂房沒了
  • refundable deposit — 押金，會退
CHUNK 3 · 約 4 分鐘

從機場到市區

Bangkok has two airports. Suvarnabhumi (BKK) is the main international one; Don Mueang (DMK) mostly handles low-cost carriers. They are an hour apart, so check which one you are landing at before you book anything.

From Suvarnabhumi, the Airport Rail Link runs to Phaya Thai station and connects to the BTS Skytrain. It is cheap and avoids traffic, but it stops running around midnight.

Could you use the meter, please?

How much to Sukhumvit, roughly?

I’d rather take the meter, thanks.

CHUNK 4 · 約 4 分鐘

飯店櫃檯

Hi, I have a reservation under the name Chen.

Certainly. May I see your passport, please?

Here you go.

Thank you. We have you in a twin room for three nights, checking out on Tuesday.

That’s right. Is breakfast included?

Yes, from 6:30 to 10:00 on the second floor.

Great. And what time do I need to check out?

By midday. If you need a late check-out, let us know the night before.

One more thing — could I leave my bags here after check-out?

Of course. The luggage room is just behind you.

Here you go. — 遞東西給對方最自然的一句，比 “Give you.” 自然一百倍。

Day 2 — 9/11（五）吃、買、出狀況

明天出發。今天讀的東西，48 小時內驗收。
CHUNK 1 · 約 4 分鐘

餐廳

Table for two, please.

Do you have a reservation?

No, we don’t. Is that all right?

That’s fine, it’ll be about ten minutes. Would you like to wait at the bar?

Sure, thanks.

菜單常見字：stir-fried（快炒）· grilled（燒烤）· steamed（清蒸）· deep-fried（油炸）· minced（絞碎）· served with（附上）

泰國餐廳的 mild 跟你的標準不一樣。 真的不吃辣：No chilli at all, please.
CHUNK 2 · 約 4 分鐘

夜市與殺價

Bargaining is expected at markets like Chatuchak or the night markets, but not in malls or convenience stores. The tone matters more than the words — stay friendly, smile, and be willing to walk away.

How much is this?

Five hundred baht.

Hmm, that’s a bit more than I wanted to spend.

How much do you want?

Would you take three hundred?

Three fifty, and that’s my best price.

Okay, I’ll take it. Could you throw in one of those?

  • That’s a bit more than I wanted to spend. 比 “Too expensive!” 有禮貌得多
  • Would you take 300? 出價的標準說法
  • I’ll think about it, thanks. 走人，常被叫回來
  • Could you throw in …? 能不能再送我一個…
CHUNK 3 · 約 4 分鐘

便利商店與藥局

7-Eleven is everywhere in Bangkok, and staff usually speak enough English for the basics. Pharmacies are a different matter: the pharmacist will often understand written words better than spoken ones, so showing them the word on your phone works well.

Do you have a plug adapter?

Could I get a bag, please?

Can I top up my SIM card here?

I’ve got an upset stomach.

I’ve been bitten by mosquitoes. Do you have something for the itching?

Is this drowsy or non-drowsy?

💻 公司電腦精讀（30 分鐘）

Refund and Exchange Policy

Goods purchased at our stalls may be exchanged within seven days of purchase, provided that the item is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the receipt. Items reduced in a sale are sold as seen and cannot be exchanged unless they are found to be faulty.

Refunds are issued in the original form of payment. Where payment was made in cash, refunds over 2,000 baht may be issued by bank transfer instead, and may take up to five working days to reach your account. We are unable to refund the cost of alterations under any circumstances.
Q1. 買了特價襯衫，三天後不喜歡想換。可以嗎？為什麼？
Q2. 同一件特價襯衫，回飯店發現有破洞。這次可以換嗎？哪一句支持你的判斷？
Q3. 用現金買 3,000 泰銖的東西要退款，會發生什麼事？多久拿得到？
Q4. sold as seen 依上下文推測是：
(a) 只賣展示品　(b) 買了就是這樣，不接受退換　(c) 需當場檢查　(d) 現貨供應
Q5. 第一句 provided that … 後面接了三個條件，分別列出來。
D1. 不認識的字全部列出
D2. 兩天共 24 句，哪幾句你真的說得出口？哪幾句看得懂但一定卡住？
D2 特別重要 — 那是驗證「輸入端句型」策略有沒有效的唯一依據。
