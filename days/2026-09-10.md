> 主題：入境 → 交通 → 飯店 check-in
> 48 小時後你就會在曼谷用到這些。讀的時候請把場景想出來。

## 🚇 通勤泛讀（30 分鐘 · 4 個 chunk）

**規則：不查字典。看不懂就跳過，把整塊讀完。圈起來的字最後統一處理。**

### Chunk 1 — 入境卡與海關（約 4 分鐘）

Thailand no longer requires most visitors to complete a paper arrival card, but you
should still be ready to answer the same questions at the counter. Officers ask them
quickly and they rarely repeat themselves.

> **Purpose of visit?** — Tourism.
> **How long will you be staying?** — Four nights.
> **Where are you staying?** — At the Ibis Bangkok Riverside. I have the booking here.
> **Do you have a return ticket?** — Yes, I fly back on Tuesday.

Signs you will pass on the way:

- **Passport Control** / **Immigration** — 查驗護照的地方
- **Thai Nationals** vs **Foreign Passports** — 站錯排會被請回去
- **Baggage Claim** — 領行李
- **Nothing to Declare** (green) vs **Goods to Declare** (red) — 海關兩條通道
- **Customs** — 海關本身

A common instruction on the screens: *"Please have your passport and boarding pass
ready."* — **have something ready** 是「先拿在手上」，不是「擁有」。

### Chunk 2 — 訂房確認信（約 5 分鐘）

真實的訂房信長這樣。**細節都藏在中段。**

> Dear Mr Chen,
>
> Thank you for choosing Ibis Bangkok Riverside. Your reservation is confirmed.
>
> **Check-in:** from 14:00 on Saturday 12 September
> **Check-out:** by 12:00 on Tuesday 15 September
> **Room:** 1 Standard Twin, non-smoking, breakfast included
>
> Early check-in is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed. If you expect to
> arrive after 18:00, please inform us in advance; otherwise the room may be released.
> A refundable deposit of THB 1,000 will be taken at check-in and returned on departure,
> provided the room is left in good condition.
>
> Free cancellation until 23:59 on 10 September. After that, the first night will be
> charged.

三個你一定要看懂的詞：

- **subject to availability** — 「看有沒有空房」＝ 不保證
- **released** — 房間被「釋出」給別人，也就是你的訂房沒了
- **refundable deposit** — 押金，會退

### Chunk 3 — 從機場到市區（約 4 分鐘）

Bangkok has two airports. **Suvarnabhumi (BKK)** is the main international one;
**Don Mueang (DMK)** mostly handles low-cost carriers. They are an hour apart, so
check which one you are landing at before you book anything.

From Suvarnabhumi, the **Airport Rail Link** runs to Phaya Thai station and connects to
the BTS Skytrain. It is cheap and avoids traffic, but it stops running around midnight.

If you take a taxi, insist on the meter:

> **Could you use the meter, please?**
> **How much to Sukhumvit, roughly?**
> **I'd rather take the meter, thanks.**

Grab (the local ride-hailing app) shows the fare in advance, which removes the
argument entirely. Drivers may call you; a typical message reads *"I am waiting at
Gate 4, level 1."*

### Chunk 4 — 飯店櫃檯（約 4 分鐘）

> **Hi, I have a reservation under the name Chen.**
> Certainly. May I see your passport, please?
> **Here you go.**
> Thank you. We have you in a twin room for three nights, checking out on Tuesday.
> **That's right. Is breakfast included?**
> Yes, from 6:30 to 10:00 on the second floor.
> **Great. And what time do I need to check out?**
> By midday. If you need a late check-out, let us know the night before.
> **One more thing — could I leave my bags here after check-out?**
> Of course. The luggage room is just behind you.

注意 **Here you go.** — 遞東西給對方時最自然的一句，比 "Give you." 自然一百倍。

## 💻 公司電腦精讀（30 分鐘）

### Part A — 句型卡（10 分鐘）

抄一遍，或至少逐句唸過（心裡唸也行）。**加粗的是骨架，其他可以換。**

**入境**
1. **I'm here for** tourism. / **I'm here on** holiday.
2. **I'm staying for** four nights.
3. **I'm staying at** the Ibis Bangkok Riverside.
4. **I have** the booking **on my phone**, if you need it.

**計程車 / Grab**
5. **Could you use the meter, please?**
6. **How much to** Sukhumvit, **roughly**?
7. **Could you drop me off** at the corner?
8. **Sorry, I think we've missed the turning.**

**飯店**
9. **I have a reservation under** Chen.
10. **Is breakfast included?**
11. **Could I have a late check-out?**
12. **Could I leave my bags here** until five?

> 🔑 `Could you...?` / `Could I...?` 是這整趟旅程最有用的兩個開頭。
> 它禮貌、萬用、而且不會出錯。**不確定怎麼說的時候，一律 `Could I ... ?`**

### Part B — 精讀（15 分鐘）

再讀一次 Chunk 2 的訂房確認信，**這次慢慢讀、拆句子**。然後回答：

**Q1.** 你的班機延誤，預計 20:30 才到飯店。根據信件內容，你**必須**做什麼？
不做的話會發生什麼事？

**Q2.** 你 9/11 下午決定不去了，取消訂房。要付錢嗎？付多少？

**Q3.** 下面這句的主要子句是哪一段？`provided` 在這裡是什麼意思？
> A refundable deposit of THB 1,000 will be taken at check-in and returned on
> departure, provided the room is left in good condition.

**Q4.**（判斷題）"Early check-in is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed."
　　這句話等於下列哪一個？
　　(a) 提早入住要加錢
　　(b) 提早入住不一定可以，看當天有沒有空房
　　(c) 提早入住需要事先申請
　　(d) 提早入住只限特定房型
