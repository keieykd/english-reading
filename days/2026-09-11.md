## 🚇 通勤泛讀（30 分鐘 · 4 個 chunk）

**規則不變：不查字典，讀完最重要。**

### Chunk 1 — 餐廳（約 4 分鐘）

> **Table for two, please.**
> Do you have a reservation?
> **No, we don't. Is that all right?**
> That's fine, it'll be about ten minutes. Would you like to wait at the bar?
> **Sure, thanks.**

點餐時最常用到的幾句：

> **Could we have a menu in English, please?**
> **What would you recommend?**
> **Is this dish spicy?** — 在泰國這句話會救你一命
> **Not spicy, please. / Just a little spicy.**
> **Does this contain peanuts?**
> **Could we get the bill, please?**
> **Can I pay by card?**

菜單上常見的字：**stir-fried**（快炒）、**grilled**（燒烤）、**steamed**（清蒸）、
**deep-fried**（油炸）、**minced**（絞碎的）、**served with**（附上）。

一個泰國特有的坑：**"mild"** 在泰國餐廳的標準跟你的標準不一樣。
如果你真的不吃辣，說 **"No chilli at all, please."** 比 "mild" 安全得多。

### Chunk 2 — 夜市與殺價（約 4 分鐘）

Bargaining is expected at markets like Chatuchak or the night markets, but not in malls
or convenience stores. The tone matters more than the words — stay friendly, smile, and
be willing to walk away.

> **How much is this?**
> Five hundred baht.
> **Hmm, that's a bit more than I wanted to spend.** *(不是說「太貴」，是說「超出我的預算」)*
> How much do you want?
> **Would you take three hundred?**
> Three fifty, and that's my best price.
> **Okay, I'll take it. Could you throw in one of those?**

有用的說法：

- **That's a bit more than I wanted to spend.** — 比 "Too expensive!" 有禮貌得多
- **Would you take 300?** — 出價的標準說法
- **What's your best price?** — 請對方直接給底價
- **I'll think about it, thanks.** — 走人時的標準句，常常會被叫回來
- **I'll take two. Any discount?** — 買多殺價
- **Could you throw in ...?** — 「能不能再送我一個…」

---

### Chunk 3 — 便利商店與藥局（約 4 分鐘）

7-Eleven is everywhere in Bangkok, and staff usually speak enough English for the
basics. Pharmacies are a different matter: the pharmacist will often understand written
words better than spoken ones, so **showing them the word on your phone works well.**

> **Do you have a plug adapter?**
> **Could I get a bag, please?** *(泰國塑膠袋要另外要)*
> **Do you take card?**
> **Can I top up my SIM card here?**

藥局：

> **I've got an upset stomach.** — 拉肚子／腸胃不舒服
> **Do you have anything for motion sickness?** — 暈車暈船
> **I've been bitten by mosquitoes. Do you have something for the itching?**
> **Is this drowsy or non-drowsy?** — 這個會不會想睡
> **How many times a day should I take it?**

常見標示：**twice daily**（一天兩次）、**after meals**（飯後）、
**do not exceed the stated dose**（不可超過建議劑量）、
**may cause drowsiness**（可能引起嗜睡）。

### Chunk 4 — 出狀況（約 4 分鐘）

這一塊你最不希望用到，但一旦用到就是最重要的一塊。

**多收錢 / 帳單有問題**
> **Sorry, I think there's a mistake on the bill.**
> **I was charged twice for this.**
> **We didn't order this.**
> **Could you check it again, please?**

**東西掉了**
> **I think I left my bag in the taxi.**
> **I've lost my phone. Is there a lost and found here?**
> **Could you help me call this number?**

**迷路 / 走錯**
> **Excuse me, am I going the right way for the BTS station?**
> **Sorry, I don't understand. Could you say that again, more slowly?**
> **Could you write it down?**

**求助**
> **Could you help me, please?**
> **I need to get to a hospital.**
> **Does anyone here speak English?**

> 🔑 一個關鍵原則：**出狀況時句子要短。**
> 慌張的時候長句一定會垮，短句才說得出口，而且對方更聽得懂。

## 💻 公司電腦精讀（30 分鐘）

### Part A — 句型卡（10 分鐘）

**餐廳**
1. **Table for** two, **please.**
2. **Is this** spicy?　→　**No chilli at all, please.**
3. **Could we get the bill, please?**
4. **Can I pay by card?**

**殺價**
5. **How much is this?**
6. **That's a bit more than I wanted to spend.**
7. **Would you take** 300?
8. **I'll think about it, thanks.**

**商店／藥局**
9. **Do you have anything for** an upset stomach?
10. **How many times a day should I take it?**

**出狀況**
11. **Sorry, I think there's a mistake on the bill.**
12. **Could you say that again, more slowly?**

> 🔑 昨天的兩個萬用開頭今天繼續用：**`Could you ...?` / `Could I ...?`**
> 今天多一個：**`Do you have ...?`** — 買東西、找東西、問藥，全部通吃。

### Part B — 精讀（15 分鐘）

慢慢讀這段，拆句子，然後作答。

> **Refund and Exchange Policy**
>
> Goods purchased at our stalls may be exchanged within seven days of purchase,
> provided that the item is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the
> receipt. Items reduced in a sale are sold as seen and cannot be exchanged unless
> they are found to be faulty.
>
> Refunds are issued in the original form of payment. Where payment was made in cash,
> refunds over 2,000 baht may be issued by bank transfer instead, and may take up to
> five working days to reach your account. We are unable to refund the cost of
> alterations under any circumstances.

**Q1.** 你買了一件**特價**襯衫，三天後覺得不喜歡想換。可以嗎？為什麼？

**Q2.** 同一件特價襯衫，回飯店才發現有破洞。這次可以換嗎？哪一句支持你的判斷？

**Q3.** 你用現金買了 3,000 泰銖的東西要退款。可能會發生什麼事？多久拿得到錢？

**Q4.** `sold as seen` 這個片語，依上下文推測是什麼意思？
　　(a) 只賣展示品　(b) 買了就是這樣，不接受退換　(c) 需要當場檢查　(d) 現貨供應

**Q5.**（結構題）第一句 `provided that ...` 後面接了三個條件。把三個條件分別列出來。
